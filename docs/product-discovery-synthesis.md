# Anonymized Product Discovery Synthesis

This document keeps product reflections from professional authoring research.
It omits participant names, exact interview dates, personal record links, project
titles, and quotations. Identifiable source records stay in Drive, outside this
public repository. These observations are partial signals, not a transcript or
validated consensus.

## Product reflections

### Continuous writing across story structure

Long passages and sequences of dialogue may be cumbersome to author through a
graph-first workflow. Explore a document-like projection over existing
Interactions and test whether authors can move between continuous writing and
graph structure while preserving one canonical Story. A brief adjacent signal
about dialogue segmentation is too underspecified to count as confirmation of
this workflow need.

### Persistent notes and review discussions

Authors may need durable context such as lore, production guidance, ideas, and
open questions near the work. Existing threaded review discussions have replies
and resolution state; persistent notes have a different lifecycle. Explore
useful note categories, targets, visibility, and discovery before choosing a
persistence model or merging the two concepts.

### Reusable authoring patterns

Repeated setup may be reduced by simple presets for common objects and narrative
structures. Validate which patterns recur and how copying should handle stable
identifiers, variables, and references before considering user-authored preset
libraries.

### Non-executable reference context

Authors may benefit from arranging references and relationships without creating
an executable story path. Compare that need with existing Groups, entities,
annotations, and Story Canvas navigation. Any documentary links must remain
visibly distinct from executable Triggers.

## Current product fit

- Tiptap/ProseMirror edits one Interaction body at a time. The proposed source
  editing mode does not yet provide a continuous multi-Interaction workflow.
- The Story Canvas represents executable Interactions and Triggers. Groups
  organize Interactions as navigation metadata; they are not free-form reference
  boards.
- Anchored review comments support threads, replies, and open/resolved state.
  Standalone author notes are not implemented.
- Entities and Simulation provide reusable story context and validation, but
  there are no reusable narrative structure presets.

## Research guidance

The roadmap treats these as discovery topics, not implementation commitments.
Observe the workflows in guided sessions, keep the canonical story model intact,
and prioritize a prototype only when its frequency, friction, or risk is clear.
Do not infer product differentiation from a feature checklist; compare author
effort, scale, and coherence across representations.
